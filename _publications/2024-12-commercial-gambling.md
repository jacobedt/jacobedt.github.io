In this paper, we explore commercial casino revenue trends in the United States using data from 27 states spanning January 2012 to February 2024 (UNLV University Libraries, 2024). We focus on two primary questions: (1) How do pre- and post-pandemic revenues compare? and (2) What are projected future trends in national casino revenues? Using change point analysis, we compare pre- and post-pandemic revenues and find significant diﬀerences (p=.003). To forecast future trends in revenues, we account for pandemic-related disruptions and employ various time series models. Our results suggest that, while revenues have increased post-pandemic, future growth is likely to flatten over the next four years. These findings provide insights into the long-term recovery and sustainability of the commercial gambling industry.
